      <h2>🗣️ Ask</h2>

    </div>

    <div class="step">
      <h2>🔧 Tool: get_services</h2>
      <p>Use the customer's request to find the closest matching service offered by the business. Return the exact <code>service_id</code> for the selected service.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="step">
      <h2>❓ If</h2>
      <div class="branch success">
        <h2>✅ Matching service found</h2>
        <p>Call <code>set_agent_data</code>.</p>
        <p>Save <code>appointment_service_id</code> using the exact <code>service_id</code> returned by <code>get_services</code>.</p>
        <p>Then continue to the next appointment step.</p>
      </div>

      <div class="branch fail">
        <h2>❌ No matching service found</h2>
        <p>Politely explain that the requested service is not offered by the business. Be empathetic and conversational, then ask if there is another service they would like to book instead.</p>
        <p>After the customer gives another service request, call <code>get_services</code> again and repeat the service check.</p>
      </div>
