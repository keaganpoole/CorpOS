1. Information We Collect
1.1 Information You Provide Directly

Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your email address and password. Your password is stored securely.

Profile Information: You may provide your name and optional details to personalize your experience. If you cancel a subscription, we may collect your reason for cancellation.

Device Information: To provide AI assistance through our "Breezy" helper, we collect device type and operating system information. You may optionally provide additional context for AI assistance.

Password Vault Data: If you use the Key Vault, you provide and store sensitive account credentials and related notes. These are access-restricted, with proper encryption planned for future implementation.

AI Chat Data: Messages exchanged with the AI, conversation identifiers, and optional conversation titles are collected.

Payment Information: Subscriptions are processed via Stripe. We do not store credit card numbers or billing addresses. We store payment-related identifiers and subscription status.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

Usage Data: Information about your interactions with the Service, such as features used, pages visited, and session duration.

Cookies and Local Storage: Used to maintain sessions, remember preferences, and for authentication.
